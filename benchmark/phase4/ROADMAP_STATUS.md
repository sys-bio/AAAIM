Personal review data remain in the original ignored review_work/ and are not copied into public Phase 4 artifacts. Historical protocol and all frozen outputs remain unchanged.

Phase 4: completed the predeclared cached routing study, confidence/abstention diagnostics, cluster-aware intervals, subgroup tables, plots, method selection, and synthetic runner tests. Full-catalog retrieval already exists; no redundant retrieval component or new learned model was added. Three component-agreement/margin gates preserve 102/163 exact correct and 124/163 answered, but save only 21, 5, and 1 of 163 simulated grounded calls. None meets the prespecified minimum 20% call reduction. The selected method remains grounded selection on every reaction over frozen BM25 + epoch-1 bi-encoder RRF Top-10 evidence. This is a bounded negative routing result, not completion of every uncertainty/calibration/router proposal in the roadmap.

The strongest retrieval baseline yields 93/163 exact correct with full coverage; grounded yields 102/163 exact correct and 102/124 correct among answered. A fixed post-LLM confidence filter loses one correct answer and saves no calls. Confidence is not calibrated; grounded correctness-detection AUROC among answered cases is about 0.498, and negative-margin reference-absence AUROC is about 0.515. These reused-validation diagnostics do not support adding that confidence filter or an absence detector as a new final component.

The older same-candidate reranker matrix and optional cross-encoder are not established by these results. Broader roadmap wording remains a separate scope reconciliation; goals.md and manuscript files were not edited. Train-based calibration, stronger routers, all-969 grounded validation, synthetic answer-absence stress tests, and independent review remain optional extensions, not prerequisites invented for this study.
