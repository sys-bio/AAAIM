# MODEL2507280001 annotation report

Run date: 2026-09-15  
Outcome: 508/539 locally scorable metabolite predictions are supported after ontology and manual identity review. Eighteen are incorrect or incomplete and 13 reference species have no candidate.

## Model and evaluation setup

This *Buchnera aphidicola* metabolic model has **593 species and 541 reactions**. The run used forced chemical typing, ChEBI, direct retrieval with `top_k=3`, and final `n_return=1`. The publication supplement was withheld during prediction and used afterward through its metabolite names, formulas, charges, and KEGG identifiers.

The final LLM ranking call returned an empty response, so the saved CSV uses deterministic retrieval rank 1. The following results therefore assess retrieval top 1 rather than a successful LLM re-ranking.

## Important limitations and next steps

- The installed ChEBI→KEGG mapping undercounts correct charge, protonation, and alternate ontology forms. It should not be used as the sole definition of correctness.
- Formula and charge from the SBML FBC extension should be added to candidate validation. This would reject acetate ester, formate ester, carbon dioxide for cobalt, and free acids for ACP-bound species.
- ACP-bound compounds, phospholipids, peptidoglycan intermediates, and folate conjugates remain the main no-candidate or incomplete categories.
- Investigate the empty ranking response before calling this an LLM-ranked result. Top 3 should remain a secondary sensitivity analysis.
