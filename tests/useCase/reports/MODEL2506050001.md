# MODEL2506050001 annotation report

Run date: 2026-09-15  
Outcome: 153/168 predictions on publication-mapped nodes are supported after accounting for valid human/mouse/rat orthologs. Nine rows are identity mismatches and six APC/C-subunit rows remain related but not reference-matched.

## Model and evaluation setup

This hypoxia/EMT qualitative model has **165 qualitative species and 165 transitions**. The run forced `entity_type="gene"` and NCBI Gene for human (`9606`), mouse (`10090`), and rat (`10116`). It used direct retrieval with `top_k=3` and retained one biological identity per species or complex component (`n_return=1`). One identity may produce as many as three Entrez rows—one ortholog per requested taxon.

The publication `.dmms` `NodeGenes` lists are useful but incomplete across taxa. Evaluation therefore distinguishes an identifier copied exactly from `NodeGenes` from a verified ortholog of the same named gene in a requested taxon.

## Related but not reference-matched

For `pAPC`, AAAIM predicted **ANAPC4** (29945, 52206, 305420) and **ANAPC11** (51529, 66156, 498030) for human, mouse, and rat. These are real APC/C subunits, but the source mapping specifically uses **ANAPC1** (64682, 17222, 311412) and **ANAPC2** (29882, 99152, 296558). The predictions are biologically related but do not recover the deposited components, so they are excluded from the 153 supported rows pending review.

## Important limitations

- The 245-ID recall denominator contains many multi-gene families and complexes. It measures recovery of an entire reference list and will remain lower under top 1 even when the selected identity is correct.
- Of 114 nodes without a source `NodeGenes` mapping, 109 receive predictions under forced-gene mode. They are not included in precision because there is no identifier-level reference.
- A later top-3 sensitivity run is useful for family/complex coverage, particularly RTK, PI3K, ORC, mTORC2, and APC/C. Top 1 remains the primary precision-oriented result.
